## kind

Can you see his yellow face and his white feathers? This is an Egyptian vulture. He mostly eats dead animals. Did you know he uses stones? He throws them at big eggs to break them open!

## b1

The Egyptian vulture lives in southern Europe, Africa and Asia. He likes dry plains and low hills. He mostly eats dead animals. He also eats dung and sometimes small animals or eggs. His body is white and his big wing feathers are black. His face is yellow. There are fewer and fewer Egyptian vultures. They get poisoned or fly into power lines.

## kenner

Neophron percnopterus is an Old World vulture. It is an opportunistic scavenger and the only Old World vulture that regularly eats dung. It cracks big eggs by throwing a small stone at them, a well-known example of tool use in birds. The clutch is usually two eggs, incubated for about 42 days. The range runs from the Iberian Peninsula and North Africa to India. The IUCN considers the species Endangered, with a decreasing trend. Poisoning, including by diclofenac, collisions with power lines and hunting are the main causes.
