## kind

Can you see his tail with the black and white rings? This is a ring-tailed lemur. He comes from the island of Madagascar. Did you know he has stink fights? The males rub their tails with scent and wave them at each other!

## b1

The ring-tailed lemur lives only on Madagascar, in the south and south-west. He lives in dry forest and along rivers. His body is about 39 to 46 centimetres long, and his tail is even longer. The tail has black and white rings. He eats fruit and leaves, especially from the tamarind tree. Sometimes he also eats flowers or insects. He is endangered, because his forest is disappearing and because he is hunted and sold as a pet.

## kenner

Lemur catta is endemic to the south and south-west of Madagascar. It lives in dry forest, gallery forest, scrub and montane forest. It is an opportunistic omnivore, with tamarind as a seasonally important food, supplemented with leaves, flowers, insects and small vertebrates. Males hold stink fights, in which they rub their tails with secretions from glands on the arm and wave them at rivals. Gestation lasts 130 to 144 days and usually a single young is born. The species is classed as Endangered, with a decreasing trend, owing to habitat loss, hunting for meat and the pet trade.
