## kind

The secretarybird is a bird of prey on long legs. He walks through the grass to hunt. He catches grasshoppers, mice and even snakes! On his head are feathers that look like pens tucked behind an ear.

## b1

The secretarybird lives in Africa, south of the Sahara. He is a bird of prey, but he mostly hunts on foot. He walks through open grassland and looks for insects, rodents, lizards and snakes. He is 90 to 130 centimetres tall. He has long legs and a grey body with black "trousers". The female lays 1 to 3 eggs. She sits on them for about 42 to 46 days. The species is getting rarer, because there is less and less open grassland.

## kenner

Sagittarius serpentarius is a ground-hunting raptor and the only species in its family. It occurs in Africa south of the Sahara, in open savannah, grassland and shrubland with short grass. It is 110 to 150 cm long, 90 to 130 cm tall, weighs 2.3 to 4.3 kg and has a wingspan of 190 to 210 cm. The clutch is 1 to 3 eggs, incubated for 42 to 46 days. The diet is insects, rodents, reptiles and occasionally birds. A wild bird is estimated to live 10 to 15 years, and up to about 19 years in captivity. The species is Endangered with a rapidly decreasing trend; habitat fragmentation, overgrazing and woody encroachment are the causes.
