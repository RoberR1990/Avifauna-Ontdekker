## kind

Can you see this red panda? He has a long ringed tail and a white face. Did you know he eats almost only bamboo? In one day he can eat more than 1.5 kg of fresh leaves!

## b1

The red panda lives in the mountain forests of the Himalaya. He lives at about 2,000 to 4,300 metres above sea level. He is about as big as a large cat. His fur is reddish brown and his belly is black. He mostly eats bamboo. Sometimes he also eats fruit or eggs. The red panda is endangered, because a lot of forest is disappearing. People also hunt him.

## kenner

Ailurus fulgens is a carnivoran (order Carnivora) of the family Ailuridae, found in the eastern Himalaya and neighbouring parts of China. Even so, its diet consists largely of bamboo. Females usually give birth to one to four cubs after a gestation of a little over four months. Wild animals live about 8 to 10 years, those in captivity 13 to 14 years. The IUCN classes the species as Endangered with a decreasing trend, owing to deforestation, habitat fragmentation, poaching and livestock.
