## kind

The night heron is a heron that mostly goes hunting in the dark. He rests during the day and wakes up in the evening. He stands very still by the water and snaps up a little fish in a flash. Can you see his black cap?

## b1

The night heron is a small, stocky heron. He lives almost everywhere in the world, near marshes, rivers and lakes. He has a black crown and back, grey wings and a white belly. He is 58 to 72 centimetres long. He hunts mostly from the evening until early morning. Then he catches fish, frogs and insects. The female usually lays 3 to 5 eggs. Sitting on them takes 24 to 26 days.

## kenner

Nycticorax nycticorax has a near-worldwide range, occurring in Europe, Asia, Africa and North and South America at fresh and salt-water wetlands. It nests in colonies in trees or reeds and on islets. Length is 58 to 72 cm, mass 520 to 1010 g and wingspan 105 to 118 cm. The clutch is usually 3 to 5 eggs, incubated for 24 to 26 days. The diet is mainly fish, supplemented with amphibians, crustaceans, insects and small vertebrates, preferably hunted at dusk and by night. A wild individual lived more than 21 years. The species is listed as Least Concern; pesticides such as DDT once did much harm, and conflicts at fish farms still occur.
