## kind

Can you see this big vulture with his bald head? He cleans up dead animals in Africa. Did you know a Rüppell's vulture has been seen at 11,300 metres? That is higher than any other bird!

## b1

Rüppell's vulture lives in Africa, in the Sahel and in the east of the continent. He is a scavenger. This means he eats dead animals, such as wildebeest and zebras that have died. In this way he keeps the savanna clean. His wings are 220 to 260 centimetres wide. He breeds in groups on high cliffs and lays only one egg. This vulture is critically endangered. Many vultures die from poison meant for other animals.

## kenner

Gyps rueppelli is an Old World vulture (Accipitridae) from the Sahel and East Africa, breeding in colonies on cliffs. The pair lays a single egg, incubated for about 55 days, and the young stay in the nest for around five months. As an obligate scavenger, the species follows herds of large hoofed animals. It is regarded as the highest-flying bird on record, with an observation at 11,300 metres. The IUCN classes it as Critically Endangered with a decreasing trend, mainly because of poisoning (including carbofuran), habitat loss, hunting for traditional medicine and a shrinking food supply.
