## kind

This big white bird has a red beak and red legs. He flies far, all the way to Africa! On the way he prefers to be carried by warm air that rises up. Gliding costs him 23 times less fat than flapping.

## b1

The white stork is a big white bird with black flight feathers, a red beak and red legs. It breeds in Europe, north-west Africa and western Asia and spends the winter mainly in Africa. It looks for food on grassland, farmland and in shallow water. It eats insects, earthworms, frogs, fish and small mammals. The female usually lays 3 to 6 eggs. In the 19th and 20th centuries the stork did badly. Now numbers are growing again, partly because of reintroduction programmes.

## kenner

Ciconia ciconia is 100 to 115 cm long, 100 to 125 cm tall, weighs 2,300 to 4,500 g (some sources give higher weights) and has a wingspan of 155 to 215 cm. The clutch is usually 3 to 6 eggs and incubation lasts 30 to 37 days. Wild storks live 20 to 26 years, captive ones 35 to 48. The species breeds in Europe, north-west Africa and western Asia and in parts of southern Africa, and migrates to Africa. On migration, energy cost shapes the route: soaring flight costs 23 times less fat than flapping, per distance travelled. The IUCN status is Least Concern, with an increasing trend after the decline of the 19th and 20th centuries.
