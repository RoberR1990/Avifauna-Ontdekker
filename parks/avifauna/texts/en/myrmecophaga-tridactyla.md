## kind

Can you see his long nose and his big, fluffy tail? This is a giant anteater. He pokes his tongue into an ant nest and licks up the ants. Did you know his tongue is about 60 centimetres long?

## b1

The giant anteater lives in Central and South America. He lives in grassland, swamp and forest. His body is about one metre long, plus a big, hairy tail. He has a long snout. He eats ants and termites, up to 35,000 insects a day. He tears a nest open with his strong claws and picks up the insects with his long tongue. He is in trouble. He is losing his habitat and is hit by cars or hunted.

## kenner

Myrmecophaga tridactyla is an anteater (Myrmecophagidae) of Central and South America that lives on ants and termites. It catches them with a tongue of about 60 cm, with which it eats up to 35,000 insects a day and attacks up to 200 nests a day. Its front claws are strong enough to defend itself even against a jaguar. After a gestation of 170 to 190 days the female has a single young. The range runs from Honduras to northern Argentina. The species is listed as Vulnerable on the Red List, with a decreasing trend: up to 2014 numbers fell by more than 30% over three generations. Habitat loss, fires, traffic and hunting are the main causes.
