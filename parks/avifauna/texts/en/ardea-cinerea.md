## kind

This big bird sometimes stands very still in the water. He is waiting for a little fish or a frog. Suddenly his long neck shoots forward and he grabs his food! He can even eat an eel 57 cm long.

## b1

The grey heron is a big grey bird with long legs and a long neck. It lives all over Europe, Asia and most of Africa. You can see it at lakes, rivers, ponds and marshes. It stands still in the water and catches fish, but also frogs, insects and sometimes a small mammal. The female usually lays 3 to 5 eggs, which are incubated for 25 to 26 days. In the wild a heron lives about 5 years on average, but the oldest known one reached 23.

## kenner

Ardea cinerea breeds throughout Europe and Asia and across most of Africa, at fresh and brackish water and along sea coasts. The bird is 84 to 102 cm long, weighs 1,020 to 2,080 g and has a wingspan of 155 to 195 cm. Prey ranges from fish of 1 cm to an eel of 57 cm; besides fish it takes amphibians, crustaceans, insects and occasionally small birds and mammals. The clutch is usually 3 to 5 eggs, incubated for 25 to 26 days. Average lifespan in the wild is about 5 years, with a maximum of 23 years. The IUCN status is Least Concern with a stable trend; the losses named are nest predation, parasites and the loss of nest sites.
