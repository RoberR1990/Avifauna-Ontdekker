## kind

The helmeted guineafowl is dark with white dots, as if snow has landed on his feathers. On his head he has a hard bump, like a helmet! A female lays 6 to 15 eggs in one season. Can you spot the dots?

## b1

The helmeted guineafowl comes from Africa, south of the Sahara. He lives in open country with a few trees, such as savannah and farmland. His feathers are dark grey with white dots. On his head there is a hard bump, the helmet. He eats seeds, leaves and fruit, and also insects and snails. A female lays 6 to 15 eggs. Sitting on them takes 26 to 30 days.

## kenner

Numida meleagris is a galliform of Africa south of the Sahara, 53 to 58 cm long and weighing roughly 1.1 to 1.8 kg. It lives in open, fairly dry landscapes with scattered trees and shrubs, such as savannah and farmland. The clutch is 6 to 15 eggs, incubated for 26 to 30 days. Outside the breeding season it eats mainly seeds and grain; during breeding it feeds mostly on invertebrates. The species is Least Concern with a stable trend. Hunting and natural predators such as leopards, snakes and martial eagles play a local role.
