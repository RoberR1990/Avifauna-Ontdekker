## kind

The emu is a huge bird that cannot fly. But he can run faster than most, on his long legs! Dad emu sits on the eggs for about 50 days and hardly eats anything. Could you go that long without food?

## b1

The emu comes from Australia. He is one of the biggest birds in the world and can easily grow 150 to 190 centimetres tall. He cannot fly, but he runs fast. He eats plants, seeds and fruit, and also insects. The female usually lays 5 to 24 big green eggs. The male sits on them and hardly eats anything during that time. Sitting on the eggs takes 48 to 56 days.

## kenner

Dromaius novaehollandiae is the only living species of the genus Dromaius, a ratite found almost everywhere in Australia, from savannah to open woodland and grassland. Body length is 139 to 164 cm, and body mass varies widely (18 to 60 kg). The clutch is 5 to 24 thick-shelled green eggs, incubated by the male for 48 to 56 days. The species is an omnivore, favouring plant material and insects. It is listed as Least Concern with a stable trend. Habitat fragmentation, road traffic and egg predation by introduced predators are the main risks.
