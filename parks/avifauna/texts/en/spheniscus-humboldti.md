## kind

This penguin cannot fly. But under water he swims so fast that it almost looks like flying! He catches small fish in the cold sea. Can you find the black band on his chest?

## b1

The Humboldt penguin lives on the coast of Peru and Chile. The water there is cold and full of fish. The penguin cannot fly, but he swims very well. He mostly eats small fish, such as anchovies. He has a black band on his chest. He nests in burrows or in cracks in the rocks. There are not many of these penguins left. Overfishing and the weather pattern El Niño are bad for them.

## kenner

Spheniscus humboldti is a penguin of the Pacific coast of South America, in Peru and Chile. It breeds on rocky coasts and small islands, in burrows in guano deposits, in caves and on cliff edges. The clutch is usually two eggs, incubated for about 41 days. The diet consists of schooling fish such as anchovy and sardine, supplemented with squid and crustaceans. The species is Vulnerable, with a decreasing trend: the world population is estimated at about 23,800 adults. Strong El Niño events have caused mass deaths in the past, and overfishing, bycatch, disturbance and feral cats and rats add to the pressure.
