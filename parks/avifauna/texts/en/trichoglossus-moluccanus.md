## kind

Can you see all those colours? Blue, green, orange and yellow! This is a rainbow lorikeet, a small parrot. He drinks the sweet nectar from flowers. Did you know his tongue has a tiny brush on it? He uses it to sweep up the pollen.

## b1

The rainbow lorikeet is a colourful parrot from Australia. He lives along the east coast, in forests, bushland and sometimes in gardens and parks. He is about 25 to 30 centimetres long. His head is blue, his back is green and his chest is orange. He mostly eats nectar, pollen and fruit. His tongue has a small brush on it, which he uses to get the nectar out of flowers. He is not threatened, and in Australia you often see him.

## kenner

Trichoglossus moluccanus is a lorikeet (Loriini) from the east coast of Australia and is a different species from T. haematodus. The tongue bears papillae, an adaptation for collecting nectar and pollen from flowering plants, including eucalyptus and Grevillea. It also eats fruit, including from crops. The clutch is one to three eggs, incubated for about 25 days. The species is listed as Least Concern on the Red List and is called the most frequently recorded bird in Australia. Outside its native range there are also introduced populations, for example in Perth, Auckland and Hong Kong.
