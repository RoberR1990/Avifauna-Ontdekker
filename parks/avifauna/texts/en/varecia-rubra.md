## kind

Can you see his red fur and black face? This is a red ruffed lemur. He lives in one small part of Madagascar and nowhere else. Did you know that? He loves to eat figs!

## b1

The red ruffed lemur is a lemur from Madagascar. He lives only in the rainforest of the Masoala peninsula. He has reddish-brown fur and a black head, belly and tail. On the back of his neck there is a white patch. He mostly eats fruit, and sometimes leaves. A mother often has two or three young. The red ruffed lemur is critically endangered. The forest is being cut down and burned. He is also hunted.

## kenner

Varecia rubra is one of the two species of the genus Varecia (Lemuridae) and occurs only in the rainforest of the Masoala peninsula in north-eastern Madagascar. The diet consists mainly of fruit, with a preference for figs; when food is scarce it adds leaves and shoots. Gestation is about 102 days and the litter is usually two or three young, occasionally up to six. In the wild the animals live 15 to 20 years. The IUCN classes the species as Critically Endangered with a decreasing trend, owing to logging, forest fires, cyclones, mining, hunting and the pet trade.
