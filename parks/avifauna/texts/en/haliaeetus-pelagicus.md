## kind

Can you see this big eagle with his yellow beak? He catches salmon in cold rivers. Did you know his wings can be up to 2.5 metres wide? That is bigger than you are!

## b1

Steller's sea eagle breeds in the east of Russia, along the coast and big rivers. In winter he flies to Japan, Korea and China. He is brownish black with white shoulders, a white tail and a big yellow beak. He mostly eats fish, such as salmon, and sometimes water birds. A female can weigh up to 9.5 kg. He usually lays two eggs. This eagle is vulnerable. There are only about 5,000 birds left, and their numbers are falling.

## kenner

Haliaeetus pelagicus is a sea eagle (Accipitridae) that breeds in the Russian Far East, from Kamchatka and the Sea of Okhotsk to the Amur basin, and winters in Japan, Korea and China. The diet consists mainly of salmonids and other fish, supplemented with water birds and carrion. The clutch is one to three eggs, incubated for 38 to 45 days. Adult plumage is reached only after about four years. The IUCN classes the species as Vulnerable, with an estimated 5,000 birds and a decreasing trend. Threats include loss of old coastal forest, pollution, overfishing, lead poisoning and persecution.
