## kind

This is the tallest bird in the world that can fly! Standing up he is as much as 1.8 metres tall, almost as big as a grown-up. His head and neck are bright red. He wades through the water on his long legs, looking for food.

## b1

The sarus crane lives in India, Southeast Asia and the north of Australia. He lives in wet places, such as marshes and rice fields. At up to 1.8 metres he is the tallest bird that can fly. His wings measure 220 to 250 centimetres from tip to tip. He has a grey body and a red head. He eats all sorts of things, such as insects, water plants, seeds and small animals. The female usually lays 1 or 2 eggs.

## kenner

Grus antigone occurs on the Indian subcontinent, in Southeast Asia and in northern Australia, in open wetlands, seasonally flooded forest and eucalyptus woodland. It is the tallest flying bird, up to 1.8 m, weighing 5 to 12 kg with a wingspan of 220 to 250 cm. The clutch is usually 1 or 2 eggs, incubated for 26 to 35 days. One captive bird reached 42 years. The species is Vulnerable; the trend differs by region, with stable or growing numbers in South Asia and Australia and a decline in Southeast Asia. Wetland loss, intensive farming, collisions with power lines and egg collection are the main threats.
