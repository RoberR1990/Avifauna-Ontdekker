## kind

This crane is white with a red patch on his head. He can live to 65, which is older than your grandparents! He lives near marshes and rivers in Asia. There he catches fish and frogs with his long beak.

## b1

The red-crowned crane lives in the northeast of Asia, in Siberia, China, Korea and Japan. He lives near marshes and rivers. He is white with black wing tips and a red patch on his head. He is 150 to 158 centimetres tall. He eats fish, frogs, insects and plants. The female usually lays 2 eggs. In the wild the bird lives 20 to 30 years, but in captivity it can reach 65.

## kenner

Grus japonensis breeds in Siberia, northeast China and Mongolia and winters in Korea and eastern China; a resident population lives on Hokkaido. It is 101 to 150 cm long (standing 150 to 158 cm), weighs 4.8 to 10.5 kg and has a wingspan of 220 to 250 cm. The clutch is usually two eggs, incubated for 30 to 31 days. The species is Vulnerable, with roughly 3,800 birds worldwide in 2020, an estimated 2,300 of them adults. Loss of wetlands to farming, river canalisation, deforestation and road building is the main threat.
