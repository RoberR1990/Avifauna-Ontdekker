## kind

This is one of the prettiest geese in the world, with a rusty-red chest and cheeks. He is black, white and red. In the wild he is clever: he builds his nest right next to a bird of prey. The bird of prey chases away the dangerous Arctic fox!

## b1

The red-breasted goose breeds in northern Siberia, on the tundra. In winter it flies to the Black Sea, to Bulgaria, Romania and Ukraine. It is small for a goose, 53 to 56 cm long, and has red patches on its chest and cheeks. It eats grass, leaves and seeds. It prefers to nest close to birds of prey, because they keep Arctic foxes away. The number of red-breasted geese is falling. Farmers change their land, hunting still happens, and almost all the geese spend the winter at just five places.

## kenner

Branta ruficollis is 53 to 56 cm long, weighs 1,050 to 1,620 g (males heavier than females) and has a wingspan of 116 to 135 cm. The breeding range lies on the Siberian tundra (Taymyr, Gyda and Yamal), the wintering range mainly along the Black Sea. The female incubates alone for 23 to 25 days, often close to nests of birds of prey as protection against Arctic foxes. The diet is grasses, leaves and seeds. The IUCN lists it as Vulnerable (downlisted from Endangered in 2015), with a decreasing trend. More than 80% of the birds winter at only five sites, which makes the species extra vulnerable to changing land use and hunting.
