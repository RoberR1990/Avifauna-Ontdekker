## kind

Can you see the messy feathers on his head? That is where his name comes from: curly crest! This is the biggest pelican in the world. He catches fish in his big throat pouch. Did you know he eats more than a kilo of fish a day?

## b1

The Dalmatian pelican is the biggest pelican. He is about 160 to 180 centimetres long. When he spreads his wings, he is more than 2.5 metres wide. He lives at big lakes and rivers from south-eastern Europe to China. He mostly eats fish, such as carp and pike. In the breeding season his throat pouch turns bright orange-red. People have drained many wetlands. That is why he is still endangered, although things are going a little better in some areas.

## kenner

Pelecanus crispus is the largest of the pelicans (Pelecanidae) and one of the heaviest flying birds. It differs from the great white pelican in its curly head feathers, pale iris and grey legs. It lives at large open wetlands from south-eastern Europe and Turkey through Central Asia to India and China; European breeders winter in the eastern Mediterranean. The clutch is usually two eggs (1 to 6) and incubation lasts 30 to 34 days. Sources differ on the current trend, so it is recorded here as unknown: one reports a decline, the other a recovery since 2005. Threats are the draining of wetlands, power lines, poaching and conflicts with fisheries.
