## kind

Can you see how pink this bird is? Did you know he turns pink because of what he eats? He strains tiny shrimps out of the water. And those shrimps make his feathers pink!

## b1

The American flamingo lives around the Caribbean Sea and in the north of South America. He lives at salty lakes and shallow lagoons. He eats tiny shrimps, worms and other small creatures from the water. That is where his pink colour comes from. A flamingo is about 120 to 145 centimetres tall. The female usually lays only one egg. This bird is not threatened, and there are even more of them now.

## kenner

Phoenicopterus ruber is a flamingo (Phoenicopteridae) from the Caribbean, the Yucatán, the Galápagos and the north of South America. The species filters food from shallow salt or brackish water; the carotenoids in its prey give the plumage its pink to red colour. Breeding colonies usually lay a single egg, incubated for 28 to 32 days, and the birds do not mature sexually until they are several years old. The IUCN rates the species as Least Concern with an increasing population. Disturbance at breeding colonies, pollution of food sources and disease remain points of concern.
