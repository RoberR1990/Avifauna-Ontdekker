## kind

Can you see the golden crown of feathers on his head? This is a grey crowned crane. He is about one metre tall. He eats seeds, insects and frogs. Did you know he can sleep in a tree? Almost no other cranes can do that!

## b1

The grey crowned crane lives in eastern and southern Africa. He lives in grassland and savanna, close to water. He is about one metre tall and has big wings. On his head he has a golden crown of feathers. He eats seeds, grass, insects, frogs and small fish. Because of the shape of his toes, he can sit and sleep in a tree. This is special for a crane. The species is endangered, because wetlands are being drained.

## kenner

Balearica regulorum is a crane (Gruidae) of eastern and southern Africa. Together with the black crowned crane (B. pavonina) it forms the only genus of cranes whose members can roost in trees, thanks to the structure of their toes. The two species can be told apart by their genetics, calls, plumage and the colour of the bare skin. The grey crowned crane is omnivorous and forages in grassland, marsh and farmland. The clutch is two to five eggs, incubated for about 28 to 31 days. In 2012 the species was moved from Vulnerable to Endangered; the total is estimated at 58,000 to 77,000 birds. Drainage of wetlands, overgrazing and pesticides are the main threats.
