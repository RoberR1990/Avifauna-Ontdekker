## kind

Can you see this red monkey? He likes to sit high up in the trees. Did you know he can roar very loudly? You can hear him from about 5 kilometres away!

## b1

The red howler monkey lives in the rainforests of South America. He spends almost the whole day high in the trees. He mostly eats leaves, and sometimes fruit and flowers. A group usually has one or two males with females and young. In the morning the whole group roars together. This tells other groups where they are. The howler monkey is not threatened yet, but numbers are going down because of hunting and the loss of forest.

## kenner

Alouatta seniculus is a howler monkey (Atelidae) from northern South America. As a leaf-eater, it has a large hindgut full of bacteria that ferment plant material. Groups are polygynous, usually with one or two adult males, and gestation lasts about 190 days. The striking dawn chorus, which carries up to about 5 kilometres, helps mark out territories and limits direct conflict between groups. The IUCN classes the species as Least Concern because of its large range, but the trend is downward owing to hunting and deforestation.
