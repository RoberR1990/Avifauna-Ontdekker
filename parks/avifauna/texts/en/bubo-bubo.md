## kind

Can you see his big orange eyes and the feathers like ears? This is an eagle-owl, one of the biggest owls in the world. He hunts at night for mice and rabbits. Did you know his wings can be 1.8 metres wide when he spreads them?

## b1

The Eurasian eagle-owl is one of the biggest owls in the world. He is about 56 to 75 centimetres long. He lives in Europe and Asia, and also in North Africa. He lives on rocks, in the mountains and at the edge of forests. At night he hunts rodents, rabbits and birds. The female usually lays two to four eggs. The eagle-owl is not threatened, but in many areas there are fewer owls. People and the loss of habitat are the cause.

## kenner

Bubo bubo is one of the largest owls in the world, with ear tufts and orange eyes. Size differs strongly between the sexes: females are heavier and weigh up to about 4.6 kg, males up to just over 3 kg. The diet consists mainly of small mammals such as rodents and rabbits, supplemented with birds and occasionally larger prey. The clutch is two to four eggs, incubated for about 34 days. The species lives in Europe and Asia and also in North Africa, mostly in rocky and mountainous country. On the Red List it is classed as Least Concern, but the trend is decreasing. Human-caused deaths and habitat loss are pushing numbers down.
