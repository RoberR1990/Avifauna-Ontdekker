## kind

Can you see that long white moustache? This little monkey is called the emperor tamarin. He is small, but his moustache is big! He lives in the trees of the rainforest and eats fruit and insects. Did you know he got his name from his moustache? It looks like the moustache of a German emperor.

## b1

The emperor tamarin is a small monkey from South America. He lives in the rainforest in the west of the Amazon region, in Brazil, Peru and Bolivia. His body is about 23 to 26 centimetres long. His tail is longer than his body. He has a long, white moustache. He eats fruit, flowers, tree sap and insects. Sometimes he also eats a frog. The monkey is not threatened yet, but the forest is getting smaller.

## kenner

Saguinus imperator is a tamarin (Callitrichidae) from the south-west of the Amazon basin, in Brazil, Peru and Bolivia. It lives in lowland rainforest, including secondary forest and forest edges, and eats a mixed diet of fruit, flowers, gum and sap, insects and small vertebrates such as frogs. Litter size is usually two and gestation lasts 140 to 145 days. In the wild it can live 10 to 20 years. The IUCN rates it Least Concern, but according to the sources the population is declining owing to deforestation and forest fragmentation.
