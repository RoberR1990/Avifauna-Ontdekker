## kind

Can you see the red patch on his forehead? And his green feathers with blue? This is a military macaw. Did you know he licks clay from rocks? It helps against poison in his food!

## b1

The military macaw is a large parrot from Central and South America. He lives in dry forests, often in the mountains. His feathers are green, with blue and yellow in the wings and tail. He has a red patch on his forehead. He mostly eats seeds, but also fruit and leaves. He lays two or three eggs. The military macaw is vulnerable. Forest is disappearing and many birds are caught for the pet trade.

## kenner

Ara militaris is a macaw (Psittacidae) with a fragmented range from Mexico to northern Argentina, split into several subspecies. The species depends on dry and semi-dry tropical forest and feeds selectively, mainly on the seeds of a small number of plant species. The clutch is two to three eggs, with an incubation period of about 26 days. Military macaws visit clay licks, probably to neutralise toxins in their food and to obtain sodium. The IUCN classes the species as Vulnerable with a decreasing trend, owing to deforestation and capture for the trade.
