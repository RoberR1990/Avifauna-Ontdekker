## kind

Can you see the pretty fan of feathers on his head? This is a pigeon, but a very big one! It is the biggest pigeon in the world. He looks for his food on the forest floor.

## b1

The Victoria crowned pigeon lives in New Guinea. He lives in lowland forest and swamp forest. He is blue-grey with a purple-red chest and a crest of lace-like feathers. Such a pigeon is about 73 to 80 centimetres long. He searches the ground for fallen fruit. The female lays only one egg. This pigeon is near threatened. The forest is being cut down and he is hunted for his feathers and his meat.

## kenner

Goura victoria is the largest living pigeon and occurs in the lowland and swamp forests of northern New Guinea and surrounding islands. It forages on the forest floor and eats mainly fallen fruit, supplemented with seeds and invertebrates. The clutch is a single egg, with an incubation period of about 30 days. The young are cared for for a long time. Two subspecies are recognised. The IUCN classes the species as Near Threatened with a decreasing trend. Habitat loss through logging and hunting for meat and feathers are the main threats; the species is listed on CITES Appendix II.
